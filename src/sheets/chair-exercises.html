Chair Exercises — Page A

All seated, all in an ordinary chair. Half of page A still counts as a full visit.

Before you start: education only, never exercise prescription. Sit tall-ish but comfortable, feet flat if they reach. If they don't, a folded towel under the feet is a fine fix. Move only with your doctor or physical therapist's clearance.
1 Seated march
Lift one knee, set it down; other knee, down. Like marching to a slow song. 10 each side, or fewer.
2 Ankle circles
Lift one foot a little; circle the ankle ten times each direction. Switch. Feet wake up first.
3 Heel and toe taps
Tap toes up, then heels up, alternating — like pumping a very lazy pedal. 15 total.
4 Arm punches
Two fists up like the world's politest boxer; punch forward, alternating arms, 10 each. Bad puns encouraged.
5 Shoulder rolls + overhead reach
Three big rolls backward, then both hands up toward the ceiling as far as comfortable — three times.

Chair Exercises — Page B

A little more for the legs, so standing up keeps being a thing you do on purpose.

Still education, not prescription. A sturdy chair that doesn't roll is the whole safety plan for this page. Chair near a wall or armrest if you'd like it nearby.
6 Sit-to-stand, chair-friendly
Stand up using the armrests or knees, sit back down gently. 3–5 times. one is a round; five is a legend.
7 Calf pumps
Feet flat, lift heels, lower. Like wiggling the legs awake at a desk. 15 slow ones.
8 Straight-leg hold
Sit tall, extend one leg until it's level, hold 5 seconds, lower slowly. 5 each side if the thigh agrees.
9 Seated side twist
Right hand to left knee, look gently over your left shoulder; breathe; switch. Range optional, twist tiny.
10 Seal it with a breath
Hands on thighs, palms down, close eyes if you like. Five slow breaths. That's the whole workout.
