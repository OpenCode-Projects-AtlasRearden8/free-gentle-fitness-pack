Walk-and-Count Mileage Log

Walking with a number to count is easier than walking with a number to reach. Count anything: steps, lampposts, songs, blocks, mailbox-and-back laps.

Approved-route reminder: count only what's yours to count — this log is education, not exercise prescription. Walk only on routes your doctor or physical therapist is comfortable with, and turn around whenever the body asks.

Why count instead of track?

A counted walk gives a number without a device, a charge, or a target. "42 lampposts" beats "I should have hit 6,000 steps." If a walk has no count, that's fine too — it still happened.

Rough distance note, if you want one: your count, repeated on the same route, is its own mileage record. Same number, same route, same story.
