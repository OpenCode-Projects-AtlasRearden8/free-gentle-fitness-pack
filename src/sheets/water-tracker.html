Water-Sip Tracker

Eight cups a day is a rumor, not a rule. Small sips, tea, soup, juice — it all counts. Tick what you actually had; leave what you didn't.

Hydration edition of the reminder: this tracker is education, not exercise prescription. Fluid amounts that suit you may differ — some people (watching certain medicines, hearts, or kidneys) are asked to limit fluids. Use the amount your doctor or physical therapist recommends, and treat this sheet as a gentle tally, not a quota.

How it works: each row is one day. Each cup is a real cup, mug, or bottle — your cup, your size. Shade or tick as you go. "Half a cup twice" is just as accurate.
