Five-Minute Wake-Up Stretch

Six easy moves, about 50 seconds each. Do them standing by your bed, in the doorway light, wherever the day finds you.

Before you start: this card is education, not exercise prescription. Move through it only if your doctor or physical therapist has cleared you to stretch — and only into whatever range feels easy today. Gentle shaking off, not forcing.
1 Reach up tall, breathe out slow
Arms as high as they comfortably go, palms up or down — your call. Ten short, unhurried breaths.
2 Shoulder rolls
Roll them up, back, and down five times forward, then five times backward. Small circles are fine.
3 Easy neck tilts
Right ear toward right shoulder; hold a few breaths. Then left. Go only as far as is comfortable — never push the neck.
4 Standing side reach
Left hand on hip, right arm up and curve gently sideways. A few slow breaths. Then switch sides.
5 March in place — small steps
Lift one knee, then the other, wherever knees go today. 10 lifts if you can, 2 if that's what there is.
6 Big sigh-out roll-down
Head heavy, chest, ribs — curl down only as far as it likes. Hang a breath. Rise slowly, stacking as you go.

Done as far as step 6? That was the whole card. Beat-up day? Steps 1–3 count as a full round.
