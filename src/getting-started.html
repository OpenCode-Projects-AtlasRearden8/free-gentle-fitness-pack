Welcome in. Take what helps; leave the rest.

This is a free printable pack for moving gently at any size, at any energy level, on any given day. No apps, no signups, no streaks to lose. Print the page you need, or all of them, and let the rest wait.

Nothing here knows your weight, your history, or your limits. Pick the sheet that matches today's energy — not the one you wish you had. Some days that's a stretch card. Some days it's the permission slip. Both count.

What's in the pack

  • Five-Minute Wake-Up Stretch — one card, six easy moves, done before the coffee cools.
  • Chair Exercises — two pages of seated movement for low-energy days.
  • Walk-and-Count Mileage Log — walk, count something, jot it down.
  • Balance Practice — everyday steadiness, near a counter, at your own pace.
  • Water-Sip Tracker — tick sips, not perfection.
  • Rest-Day Permission Slip — the official-looking excuse to not move, signed by you.
  • Movement Bingo — a monthly card of tiny wins. Any five in a row counts.

Printing tips

  • Print at 100% / "Actual size" on letter paper. It scales fine onto A4 too.
  • You keep this file; it works offline forever. Nothing is collected, nothing is tracked.
